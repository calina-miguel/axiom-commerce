# Axiom Commerce direction

## Purpose

Provide a clear, focused route for people exploring retail ai and agentic shopping.

## Experience

Keep the brand identity, palette, typography, favicon, and content specific to Axiom Commerce. Use concise headings, useful supporting copy, accessible interactions, and lightweight motion with reduced-motion support. Preserve a clear next action throughout the page.

## Boundaries

This product has its own repository, deployment, and data configuration. Keep all implementation and operational settings within this project. Do not reference another brand's account, workspace, database, or hosting settings.
